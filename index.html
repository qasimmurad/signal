    How to nudge

    • Hand pinch thumb and index to take the gripper. Move your hand to move it, bring it toward the camera to push in. Let go to hand back.
    • Voice "stop", "left a bit", "the red one", "yes".
    • Pointer rest on a control for ¾ s to send the gripper there. Works with eye-trackers and head mice.
    • Keys arrows move, W / S push in / out.

    An open hand in view with no pinch counts as a person in the workspace: the arm slows.
